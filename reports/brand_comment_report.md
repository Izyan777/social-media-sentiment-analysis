# AI-assisted improvement recommendations

Priorities are ranked by **how many negative comments** each theme accounts for. Themes marked ▲ are *statistically* more negative than the overall average (chi-square test, Bonferroni-corrected for the number of themes tested, p < 0.05).

## 1. Product quality
- Mentioned in **19** comments (47.5% of all); **63%** negative (lift ×1.15, p=0.504, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Run a quality audit on the most-complained items, add real customer photos and exact size/material details to listings, and tighten supplier QA before dispatch.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Fake product bheja gaya dhoka dene walon se door raho”
  - “Khana thanda tha aur taste bilkul bakwas”
  - “Size chota aya aur kapra bhi ghatiya hai”

## 2. Price & value
- Mentioned in **8** comments (20.0% of all); **75%** negative (lift ×1.36, p=0.382, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Reply to price complaints with clear value (what is included, warranty, bundles). Test targeted discounts or smaller pack sizes for price-sensitive cities and the sale-season calendar.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Mehngai ne kamar tod di price phir barha di”
  - “Itna mehnga hai aur quality bhi kharab”
  - “Quality bohat kharab hai 2 din mein hi toota gaya paisay zaya”

## 3. Delivery & logistics
- Mentioned in **9** comments (22.5% of all); **33%** negative (lift ×0.61, p=0.27, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Publish realistic delivery windows, add live parcel tracking, send proactive delay messages, and review courier partners by city. Track on-time delivery rate weekly.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Delivery late aur parcel damaged hai bohat afsos”
  - “Delivery boy ne call tak nahi uthayi parcel kho gaya”
  - “Parcel 10 din late pohncha aur tracking bhi kaam nahi kar rahi”

## 4. Customer service
- Mentioned in **6** comments (15.0% of all); **50%** negative (lift ×0.91, p=1, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Set a response-time promise (e.g. under 24 h), give agents Roman Urdu/Urdu templates, and make refund/return steps simple and visible. Track first-response time and resolution rate.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Bilkul bakwas customer service complaint par koi action nahi”
  - “Return ka process bohat mushkil hai koi jawab nahi deta”
  - “Customer support ne 3 din tak koi response nahi diya refund abhi tak nahi mila”

## 5. Trust & authenticity
- Mentioned in **6** comments (15.0% of all); **33%** negative (lift ×0.61, p=0.476, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Show proof of originality (authorised-dealer badge, warranty card, verified seller). Fast-track any 'fake/duplicate' complaint and publish the outcome.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Ye log nakli maal bhej rahe hain dhoka hai bilkul”
  - “Fake product bheja gaya dhoka dene walon se door raho”

## 6. Packaging
- Mentioned in **4** comments (10.0% of all); **25%** negative (lift ×0.45, p=0.458, corrected p=1).
- **Action:** Improve protective packaging for fragile items and use sealed, branded boxes so customers can tell nothing was opened or swapped.
- Example negative comments:
  - “Packing bohat kharab thi box phata hua aya”

_No theme is significantly more negative than average in this sample._
